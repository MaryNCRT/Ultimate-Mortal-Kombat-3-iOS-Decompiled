## Ultimate Mortal Kombat 3 — PC port 0.1.1 alpha (Windows)

Fixes on top of 0.1.0b: **player two's own controls for DUELO**, DUELO with every interface size, and screens that were off on wide screens with the iPad interface. As before, **no game data and no game executable are included**: you compile the game from **your own** `.ipa` of UMK3 **1.2.59 for iPhone** with `UMK3-Launcher.exe`.

**Coming from 0.1.0 or 0.1.0b:** the launcher offers this update itself (COMPILE YOUR .IPA > DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL).

### Two players on one PC
- **PLAY > DUELO is there with every interface size**, not only with the iPad's. PLAY uses the iPad's five rows.
- **CONTROLS has PLAYER 1 / PLAYER 2 tabs** on KEYBOARD and GAMEPAD. Player two gets what player one has:
  - keys for the 6- and 5-button layouts, and S;
  - a gamepad of their own;
  - two buttons per action, pause and moves list included;
  - RESET.
- Player two's keys default to the arrows and the numeric keypad.
- The two players never share a pad: with "automatic" on both, each takes a different one.
- In DUELO player two plays with their keys and pad. In every other mode both sets drive player one, as before.

### Wide screens with the iPad interface
- **Character select (16:9, 21:9):** the fighters stand beside the grid again. The iPad's select camera is used on 4:3 only.
- **Tower select (16:9, 21:9):** the difficulty labels and their torches sit on their towers again. The iPad's pulled-back tower camera is used on 4:3 only, and there the labels follow it.
- **JUGADORES (local multiplayer):** the big frame is centred.

### Known problems
- **Reptile's fatalities and specials are missing sounds**; other fighters may be too.
- After a round a fighter can be drawn with another's model (seen with Ermac against Sub-Zero).
- Some attacks seem to connect from too far.
- The red press highlight looks too thick.
- A few objects besides the fighters may still not show, and some fatalities lack animation (Sonya's).
- Some texture errors remain on some stages and fighters.
- Some modes besides Arcade can close the game.
- The game's menus are used with the mouse, not the pad.
- Windows only.
