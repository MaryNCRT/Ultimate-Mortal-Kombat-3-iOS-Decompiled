## Quick Start Guide

1. Download the launcher corresponding to your operating system.
2. Provide your legally owned copy of `Ultimate Mortal Kombat 3 (iOS v1.2.59)` in `.ipa` format.
3. Open the launcher, select your `.ipa`, and click **Build / Compilar**.
4. Configure graphics, audio, gamepad/keyboard controls, and resolution to your liking.
5. Click **Play / Jugar**!
